D1–D7 and X1 were in the record before the run; judge only lines added after them. A permission line says who may or may not do something (book, cancel, see, set); a line that only limits when or how often something happens is not one. Every added permission line names who, which action and whose or which studio's data, or cites the open question that settles the missing part. Every added line that sets what is charged or refunded names the amount, or the decision that sets it, and the event that charges or refunds it (such as booking, or cancelling 12 hours ahead). A line only about how money moves (payment method, where cash comes from) need not repeat the amount. Pass if no added line is about permissions or money.
