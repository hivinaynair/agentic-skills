# Acme Outdoor

Online outdoor-gear store on Medusa v2. Regions: EU (EUR), UK (GBP), US (USD).
Checkout takes cards through Stripe. Customers can check out as guests or with an
account. Orders can be returned within 30 days for a refund to the original payment
method. Promotions: percentage discount codes, one per order.

Product decisions: Priya (PM). Tax and accounting: the finance team.
