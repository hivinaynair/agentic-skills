# COIN-12: Pay with coins at checkout

Acceptance criteria:
1. When a customer with 120 coins pays with 30 coins: "Paid with 30 coins" shows and the balance shows 90.
2. When a customer tries to use more coins than their balance: "Enter between 1 and 120 coins" shows and the balance is unchanged.
3. When the coins field is empty and the customer pays: the same error shows and nothing is paid.
