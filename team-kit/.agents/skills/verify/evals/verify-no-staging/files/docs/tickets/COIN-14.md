# COIN-14: Pay with coins, down to zero

Acceptance criteria:
1. When a customer with 120 coins pays with 50 coins: "Paid with 50 coins" shows and the balance shows 70.
2. When the balance reaches 0: the "Pay with coins" button is disabled.
3. When a customer tries to use more coins than their balance: "Enter between 1 and <balance> coins" shows and the balance is unchanged.
