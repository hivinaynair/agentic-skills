# Decisions

- D1 accepted — Tutors submit an expense with an amount, a category and a receipt photo.
- D2 accepted — A manager approves or rejects submitted expenses; a rejection needs a reason.
- D3 accepted — Amounts are in rupees, between ₹1 and ₹50,000.
- D4 accepted — A receipt upload that fails keeps the entered amount and category.
- D5 accepted — Managers can approve several selected expenses at once.
- D6 accepted — An approved expense shows who approved it and when.
- D7 accepted — A manager cannot approve an expense they submitted; it waits for another manager.
